# Insights & Alerts

Goal: move from "here is your data" to "here is what needs attention", following the proactive-insights direction from fintech research (anomaly detection, predictive budgets, personalized nudges).

## Features

- [ ] **Unusual spending alerts** — flag any establishment/category whose spend this month deviates strongly from its historical average.
- [ ] **Top movers** — the 3 categories with the biggest increase/decrease vs previous month, with amounts.
- [ ] **"Safe to spend" widget** — given budgets remaining and days left in month, a daily safe-spend number.
- [ ] **Month-end projection** — forecast of the closing invoice total based on current run-rate.
- [ ] **Subscription price-change detection** — alert when a recurring charge's amount changes between months.
- [ ] **Subscription audit** — list recurring expenses with "keep / review / cancel" triage and total monthly commitment.
- [ ] **Recurring payment calendar** — month view with due dates of recurring charges and invoices.
- [ ] **Weekday × hour heatmap** — when you spend most (needs time data; requires parser change).
- [ ] **Year-over-year comparison** — same month last year vs this year per category.
- [ ] **Seasonality insight** — average spend per category by month of year (e.g., "Viagens peaks in January").
- [ ] **Anomaly feed** — a single "Needs attention" panel aggregating alerts, over-budget categories, unusual charges, and price hikes.
- [ ] **Spending streaks & summaries** — fun stats: biggest single expense ever, cheapest month, most frequent merchant.
- [ ] **Natural-language query** — ask "how much did I spend at restaurants this year?" and get an answer from the API data.
- [ ] **Weekly digest view** — auto-generated weekly summary page (or optional email) of the week that was.
